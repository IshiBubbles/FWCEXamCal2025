## Track 3 — British American Tobacco ⚠️ Verified: not a viable route

**BAT runs no UK school-leaver apprenticeship.** "Apprenticeship" appears only as a job-level *filter* on their search page and on aggregator profiles — there is no live UK vacancy and none announced. Precedent exists (a Mechatronics Apprenticeship in Hampshire, closed May 2024), so the watch stays passive, but there is nothing to apply for.

What BAT Southampton actually offers is **12-month undergraduate placements** (Packaging Innovation, Packaging Future Materials & Sustainability, Applied Product Science, Modern Oral Future Platforms) — all requiring **current university enrolment**. Plus a Global Graduate Programme requiring a completed degree.

**Recommendation: deprioritise.** BAT is not a viable September 2027 destination and shouldn't absorb effort belonging to the cadet schemes or STEM apprenticeships. Backdrop worth noting: the Tobacco and Vapes Act 2026 may suppress BAT's UK hiring appetite generally.
