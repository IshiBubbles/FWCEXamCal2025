## Two questions answered up front

#### 1. Nuclear Graduates — **NOT A ROUTE. Mark it "not a route".**

**Plainly: Nuclear Graduates is a post-degree graduate scheme, not a school-leaver apprenticeship.** Evidence from snippets:

- It is described as a **two-year graduate training scheme run by nuclear industry employers**, completed **after graduation**, built around **three secondments** (first with your sponsoring organisation), administered by **Energus**.
- Its discipline pages state a **minimum of a 2:2 at bachelor's level** in a relevant degree (seen explicitly on the Chemical Engineering discipline page).

There is no Level 3 / Level 6 apprenticeship entry point visible anywhere in the Nuclear Graduates material. **Recommendation: remove Nuclear Graduates from Fraser's 2027 target list entirely.** It is, however, worth a note in his long-term plan: several of the employers below (NDA, and the wider supply chain) are Nuclear Graduates *sponsors*, so it is a plausible destination *after* a degree — including after a degree apprenticeship. It is a 2032-and-later item, not a 2027 one.

*This is a clean, useful result: one of the ten is eliminated with confidence.*

#### 2. Sizewell C — **can you apply directly? Effectively no; EDF is the practical channel.**

Snippet evidence, and the nuance matters:

- **EDF provides Sizewell C with recruitment as a support service** while SZC "transitions out of the EDF group". So the recruitment *machinery* is EDF's.
- The **NEDA (Sizewell C)** and **Business Administration Apprenticeship (Sizewell C)** vacancies are listed as **EDF Energy** roles on third-party aggregators, and the stated application path is the **EDF careers site** (`edfenergy.com/careers/early-careers/apprenticeships`).
- Sizewell C *does* maintain its own **Current Vacancies** page (`sizewellc.com/jobs/current-vacancies/`), on which apprentice roles have appeared (e.g. Apprentice Quality Practitioner, Apprentice Building Services Manager). So SZC-branded postings exist — but the early-careers apprentice intake tracks EDF's January window.
- **Young SZC** is explicitly a **Suffolk and Norfolk** programme for 16–21-year-olds *living in* Suffolk and Norfolk. **Fraser is not in its catchment.** It is a registration/notification and outreach scheme, not an application route, and not one he is eligible for.

**Practical verdict:** watch the **EDF** January window and apply to NEDA with Sizewell C as a location preference if he wants Sizewell. Do not spend effort hunting a separate SZC school-leaver front door — and disregard Young SZC. Also note Sizewell is ≈ **200 miles** from Southampton (est.), so this is relocation to Suffolk regardless of channel.
