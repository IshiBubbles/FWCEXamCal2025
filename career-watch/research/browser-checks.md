# Browser-check list — the working action list

Compiled **20 August 2026**, after three research rounds covering **116 employers**.

Everything in the research dossiers came from **web-search snippets**, because the network proxy
here blocks direct page loads. That works well for "what does this company do" and "what are they
building right now". It works badly for two things: **exact person-spec wording**, which lives on
the apprenticeship page itself, and **which level a route actually is**, which snippets routinely
get wrong or attach to the wrong employer.

This file is the fix. Ten minutes in a normal browser closes gaps that no amount of further
searching will.

**How it is ordered:** by what a check *unblocks*, not by employer size. A missing **route** or
**level** can change whether Fraser applies at all, so those come before missing **wording**,
which only changes how a letter is phrased.

## Read this before trusting any requirement string

Both research rounds independently caught the same failure mode, and it is the one to watch:
**search snippets on `findapprenticeship.service.gov.uk` bleed requirements between employers.**
Generic titles like "Civil Engineering Apprenticeship (Level 6 Degree)" appeared attributed to
four different companies. Confirmed cases where attribution was refused rather than guessed:

- A "British National / security clearance" clause almost certainly belonging to **AWE** surfaced attached to **United Utilities**.
- "A Level BBC, Mathematics and preferably Chemistry or Physics" appeared under **both Mott MacDonald and Arcadis** — at least one is wrong.
- A Naval Architecture vacancy attributed to **BMT** is actually **BAE Systems, Barrow**.
- A £29.9m project attributed to **National Gas** is **SGN's**.
- **seta.co.uk** (an unrelated North East organisation) constantly contaminates results for **setatraining.co.uk** (Southampton).
- **Linde Material Handling** (forklifts) is a different business from **BOC/Linde** (industrial gases); the large "30 positions" apprenticeship is forklifts.
- **National Grid's** "communication skills, attention to detail, organisation…" list has the shape of the standard Find-an-Apprenticeship checkbox set rather than National Grid's own person spec. **Do not quote it back at them.**
- **Gradcracker serves an identical "opening Autumn 2026" template string** for Leonardo, Amentum *and* Capgemini Engineering. It is platform boilerplate, not an employer announcement — the same class of error as the BMT/BAE Barrow mix-up.
- **BAE Maritime's "251 places / 173 split"** figure traces to an article whose own title says the deadline was **February 2023**; a different piece gives 126 starters and 76 apprenticeships. Two totals, two years. Do not quote it.
- **`rina.org.uk` is the Royal Institution of Naval Architects**, not RINA Consulting. Its "Partner Universities" page surfaces in RINA searches.
- **DNV's** electro-mechanical provider is the **University of Warwick**. A University of Nottingham page for the same standard surfaced and must not be read as DNV's.
- **A systematic gap, not a one-off:** A-level grades, salary and residency wording were absent from snippets for nearly every Tier 3 employer, GE Aerospace included. **No personal-statement work should start without the live advert open in a browser.**
- £15,704 appears against two unrelated employers because it is simply the statutory apprentice minimum, not a distinguishing figure.

Where an entry carries an attribution warning, it means what it says. **Re-check any specific
figure or requirement in a browser before it goes into an application** — this is precisely the
kind of detail that gets quoted confidently and then dismantled at interview.
