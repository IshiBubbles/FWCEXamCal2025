Compiled **20 August 2026**. Currently covers Tier 1 civil only (134 employers, 494 criteria). Later rounds extend it.

## What this file is for

This answers **"which STAR moments should Fraser bank first?"** The themes at the top are asked for by the most employers, so evidence banked against them is reusable across the widest set of applications. Work down the list.

### What that means for the STAR bank

The top themes are broad behavioural qualities, not technical ones — which is good news, because Fraser's strongest material is not academic. His **FA-registered refereeing** covers decision-making under pressure, communication with hostile adults, responsibility and integrity in one activity. **Basketball** and the **summer camp helper** role cover teamwork and working with others. Those two or three moments, properly extracted, evidence most of the top half of this table.

The thinnest areas against this list are commercial awareness, and anything requiring workplace experience. Worth knowing before an interview rather than during one.
