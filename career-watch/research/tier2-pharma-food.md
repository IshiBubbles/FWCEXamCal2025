# Tier 2 — Chemical: pharma, food and FMCG

Compiled **20 August 2026** from web-search snippets; see `README.md` for the method and read the provenance tag on any criterion before quoting it.

The remaining 18 Tier 2 chemical employers. Fraser's **#2 discipline** on paper, and the slice
divided sharply: ten food and FMCG manufacturers tagged `plant` (site-based engineering
maintenance, which suits the boots-on-ground preference) against seven pharma employers tagged
`lab` (bench work, which does not), plus British American Tobacco.

---

## Where this slice landed

**One find, six miles away.** **CooperVision** is the only one of the eighteen running an
engineering apprenticeship within commuting distance — a **Level 3 Machining Technician role at
Eastleigh (~6mi)**, with **SETA** as the training provider. It sits in the pharma group but it is a
**precision manufacturer, not a laboratory**, and contact lens work is polymer and hydrogel
chemistry, so Fraser's Chemistry A-level has a real hook rather than a stretched one. They are also
building **The Vision Centre**, 107,000 sq ft at Adanac Park, completing 2026.

That is the third time **SETA** has appeared as the provider behind a local route, after ExxonMobil
Fawley. SETA is emerging as the hub for local engineering apprenticeships, which makes registering
with them worth more than it looked when it was filed as a craft-level fallback.

**Otherwise the geography question kills the slice.** No food or drink manufacturer runs its
engineering apprenticeship anywhere in Hampshire, Dorset, Wiltshire or Sussex — the closest are
**CCEP Sidcup (~90mi)** and **Britvic Beckton (~95mi)**, relocations rather than commutes.

**And the level question kills most of what is left.** Nearly every food and drink engineering route
here is **Level 3 craft, not Level 6** — Nestlé, CCEP, Britvic, PepsiCo, P&G engineering, Church &
Dwight, Syngenta Huddersfield, GSK and AstraZeneca engineering. **Unilever is the standout
exception**, with a genuine BEng Chemical Engineering via the University of Sheffield AMRC on an
A-level entry route — and it does not run in the south. So this slice yields **zero direct hits** on
"September 2027 degree apprenticeship near Southampton".

**One pharma entry where "it's lab work" is the wrong verdict: GSK Worthing (~60mi)** — Mechatronics
Technician Level 3, 12-month rotations across engineering departments, breakdown response and
fault-finding, £18,400 rising to £25,400 after year one.

#### Do not apply here

**Jacobs Douwe Egberts' Banbury site is closing** — 167 jobs, wound down by December 2026 — while
aggregators are still serving its apprenticeship listing.

#### Application seasons in this slice

Food and drink runs two distinct cycles: **Nestlé and CCEP open or close in November**, **Britvic
opens in February**, and **GSK assessment centres run February to April**. The **Cogent Skills
"Pfizer Apprenticeships 2027" register-your-interest page is already live**.

#### British American Tobacco — re-checked, nothing has changed

Still no UK school-leaver apprenticeship. Southampton remains 12-month **undergraduate** R&D
internships starting 1 September 2026 across Modern Oral, Applied Product Science and
Biotechnology, all requiring existing university enrolment.

**Bottom line on geography.**
1. **CooperVision is the only one of the 18 that runs an engineering apprenticeship within cycling distance of Fraser.** It is Level 3, not degree.
2. **GSK Worthing** is the only other genuine hands-on maintenance apprenticeship inside ~60 miles. Also Level 3.

So for a **September 2027 degree apprenticeship near Southampton**, this whole slice of 18 produces **no** direct hit. It produces two good near-misses on level (CooperVision, GSK Worthing) and one good hit on level-and-discipline that fails on geography (Unilever L6 Chemical Engineering).

**What they're working on now.** Two named, dated investments.
