### Dstl (Defence Science and Technology Laboratory)
`Tier 5` · `other` · `mixed (lab + workshop + field trials)` · **Porton Down, nr Salisbury ~35 mi from Southampton; Portsdown West, nr Fareham ~20 mi; Alverstoke, Gosport ~22 mi. Approximate road distances.**

**What they do.** Dstl is the Ministry of Defence's in-house science and technology arm — an executive agency of the MoD, staffed by civil servants, that supplies the technical evidence, experimentation and advice behind UK defence and security decisions. It is described in snippets as **"one of the world's most respected research centres and one of Salisbury's largest employers"** and as **"a government-run organisation dedicated to science and technology"**. Its remit spans chemical and biological defence, explosives and ordnance, sensors, electronics, cyber, materials, human sciences and weapons effects. Porton Down is its principal site; it also operates Portsdown West in Hampshire and Alverstoke in Gosport, and apprentice reviews reference placement time at **Fort Halstead** (Kent) — treat Fort Halstead as historic, as it has been on a long-running closure/consolidation path, and confirm current site list with a browser.

**What they're working on now.** The clearest dated live programme is **DragonFire**, the laser directed energy weapon developed by a consortium of **MBDA UK, Leonardo UK, QinetiQ and Dstl**. Timeline from snippets: successful firing trials at the **Hebrides range in October 2025**, tracking and downing drones at 650 km/h; a **£316m production contract to MBDA UK in November 2025**; and on **18 March 2026** Minister of State for Defence Vernon Coaker confirmed acceleration of the programme, bringing initial deployment forward to **2027** on **Type 45 destroyers** — a five-year pull-forward against earlier planning. Parallel land and air applications are being explored, including integration on **Wolfhound** armoured vehicles and future **GCAP** fighter aircraft. Alongside that, Dstl authored the national **Ordnance, Munitions and Explosives (OME) professional (integrated degree)** apprenticeship standard (ST0574), which tells you OME is a standing institutional priority, not a one-off.
