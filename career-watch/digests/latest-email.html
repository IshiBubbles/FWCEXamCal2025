Full refresh — Monday 17 August 2026. Treated as a first run: every entry re-verified from scratch rather than carried forward. Three previous entries turned out to be wrong and are corrected below.

⏰ The headline: the season opens in weeks, not months.

The critical window for September 2027 starts is late September → end of November 2026, with October the densest month. And several big employers close when full rather than on a deadline — Airbus says so explicitly, BAE did it in practice last year. Week-one applications matter far more than deadlines do.

✅ Seven things worth doing this month

All free, none need an application window to be open.

  1. SETA — register interest for September 2027
    The best single action available today, and I hadn't found it before. SETA is a group training association at Millbrook, inside Southampton, that places apprentices with Solent-area engineering employers and delivers their first year of training. One form puts Fraser in front of multiple local employers, and their September 2027 interest form is live right now. Zero commute problem. Craft/advanced level rather than degree, so worth weighing alongside the degree routes — but the local reach is unmatched.
  2. Create a Higherin account
    The biggest gap in the whole setup. It's the only place aggregating the live "Register Your Interest — 2027" listings (GE Aerospace, EDF, NESO, Severn Trent, E.ON, GTR and more), and it holds 48,000+ reviews written by actual apprentices — genuinely the best resource for deciding which scheme to target and for interview prep. Note it absorbed RateMyApprenticeship in August 2025, so it replaces that rather than adding to the list.
  3. UCAS Smart Alerts — best filtering of any board; the single highest-value alert signup.
  4. Amazing Apprenticeships "Vacancy Snapshot" — this is how the October bumper edition of the Higher & Degree Listing arrives automatically. Confirmed schedule: October / January / April. The October 2025 edition carried 1,600+ vacancies across 50+ employers including EDF and MBDA.
  5. GOV.UK Find an Apprenticeship — account plus a saved search: Southampton, 25 miles, all sectors, alerts on.
  6. Register interest with the employers whose 2027 pools are already open: Babcock · MBDA · EDF · AtkinsRéalis · Sellafield · UKAEA
  7. Two or three Springpod virtual work experiences — completely free, 6–10 hours each, self-paced, and the completion certificate genuinely counts on apprenticeship applications. Worth doing before October gets busy.

🔴 Three corrections — previous entries were wrong

1. The pilot apprenticeship route doesn't exist. The First Officer Pilot standard (ST0523) was retired on 1 July 2024. The tracker described it as "approved, awaiting a live vacancy" — that was wrong, and structurally there can never be one. Airline cadet schemes are the only route to a flight deck.

2. The BA practice test carries nothing forward. This is the one that matters most. The tracker recorded Speedbird as "applied, 75% through practice test." In fact the 2026 window opened 14 April and closed 23 April 2026 — four months ago. The test Fraser is part-way through sits on BA's year-round preparation page; it is not a live application and earns no standing in selection. He'll need to submit fresh in the next window, inferred at ~mid-April 2027 from four consecutive mid-April openings. That timing actually fits a September 2027 finish well — but it needs a hard diary entry, because the progress he's made doesn't hold his place.

3. TUI's cadet scheme is suspended. The tracker had it as an ongoing scheme. TUI confirmed no 2026 intake, removing ~30 funded places. Virgin's Future Flyers is also paused for redevelopment.

🎯 The strongest local options

  • ExxonMobil Fawley — ~10 miles. The closest option there is. But it's an advanced/craft apprenticeship, not degree-level, so genuinely worth weighing against what three strong A-levels could get elsewhere. Requires 18 at start of employment.
  • BAE Systems Maritime, Portsmouth — ~20 miles. The strongest local defence option by some distance: 251 additional Early Careers places announced, with 173 split between GCSE-entry and A-level-entry applicants. Royal Navy surface fleet, the carriers, Type 45 destroyers, plus radar and underwater weapons design.
  • Dstl, Porton Down — ~35 miles. Level 6 degree apprenticeships across every STEM discipline, and a confirmed February pattern. Best fit in the Civil Service track by a wide margin.
  • QinetiQ — Boscombe Down, near Salisbury. Still no information at all: no listings, no dates, no announcements. But their apprentice training school is genuinely local, so it's worth checking manually rather than writing off.
  • Arup — Southampton. Recruited both Building Services Electrical (L6) and Civil Engineering (L4) apprentices in Southampton last cycle at £27,000–£28,000 — among the highest starting salaries found anywhere in this sweep.
  • Frazer-Nash — the Level 6 cyber pathway is studied via Solent University, making it locally viable. GKN Aerospace — Isle of Wight (Cowes) route is realistic via the ferry.
  • Airbus Defence & Space, Portsmouth — I'd previously only noted Airbus's Filton and Broughton sites.

🆕 Also new since last time

Rolling routes with no window to miss — a genuine safety net, since everything else depends on hitting a narrow window. The Royal Navy Accelerated Apprentice Scheme is specifically the A-level entry route: you join as a Probationary Leading Hand, two ranks above a standard Engineering Technician entrant. Weapon Engineering and Air Engineering both suit Maths/Physics. Plus the British Army (Ammunition Technician is EOD and weapons systems work, £26,334) and the RAF (23 roles).

Employers added: Babcock, Leonardo, Thales, Sellafield, UKAEA, EDF, Dstl, MBDA, NESO, GE Aerospace, WSP, L3Harris (Portsmouth defence electronics), BAE Maritime, SETA, Arup, Royal Navy, RAF. The engineering tracker went from 8 employers to 32.

New sector portals: Careers in Defence, the ADS Group apprenticeship directory (republished each October), MOD and DE&S apprenticeships, Energy & Utilities Careers, and APTUS for power-network technical routes.

Aptitude test prep — most engineering schemes screen with an online test before interview, typically numerical, mechanical and spatial reasoning. Fraser's A-level Physics maps directly onto the mechanical reasoning sections. The usual publishers are SHL and Saville. Free practice at practiceaptitudetests.com, WikiJob and iPrep.

📉 Two tracks I'd now downgrade

British American Tobacco — not a viable route. Verified: BAT runs no UK school-leaver apprenticeship. "Apprenticeship" appears only as a filter on their job search and on aggregator profiles; there's no live vacancy and none announced. What Southampton actually offers is 12-month undergraduate placements requiring existing university enrolment. I'd stop spending effort here.

Civil Service Career Launch — Fraser is ineligible. The stated eligibility is "aged 16 or older and not in full-time education." He can't apply while still at school. It's also only a Level 3 Business Administrator qualification — a weak return on Maths/Physics/Chemistry — and Birmingham/Manchester/London only. A fallback for after he leaves school, not a target. Dstl and MI5/MI6/GCHQ are the far better Civil Service routes — the GCHQ Infrastructure Engineering apprenticeship wants three A-levels with two in STEM, which his combination satisfies cleanly.

🔎 Two things worth resolving now rather than in February

1. Fraser's nationality status. Dstl, MOD and GCHQ require UK nationality with no dual nationality plus five years' residency. AWE requires British citizenship. BAE needs 5–10 years' continuous UK residency for many roles. If dual nationality applies, a large part of the defence track closes — much better to know that now than after months of preparation.

2. His date of birth against the age gates. AWE requires 18 by 31 August 2027 for a 2027 start; ExxonMobil Fawley requires 18 at the start of employment. These are hard gates, not preferences.
