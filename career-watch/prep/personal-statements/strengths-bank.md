**Status:** Scaffold — populate in Phase 2 (September–December 2026)

**Purpose:** Three or four reusable paragraphs Fraser can drop into any application. Written once, properly, then adapted — not rewritten from scratch under deadline pressure in October.

**How to use:** Each block below is a self-contained paragraph. Pick the two or three that fit the employer and the role, adjust the opening line, and the personal statement is most of the way written.

## Notes on writing these

- **Specific beats impressive.** One real detail lands harder than three adjectives.
- **Answer the question asked.** If the form asks why *this employer*, the answer must name something only that employer does.
- **Keep each block under 150 words.** Most application forms have tight character limits.
- **Update as evidence accumulates.** Every insight day, work experience, or project is new material — add it here when it happens, not when the form is open.
