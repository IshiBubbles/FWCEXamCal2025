# Interview & Assessment Feedback Log

**Status:** Scaffold — populate from Phase 2 onward, as assessments actually happen

**Purpose:** A running record of what was asked, what landed, and what didn't. The second interview should be better than the first because of what's written here.

**How to use:** Add an entry immediately after each stage, while it's fresh. Even a bad outcome is useful material if the detail is captured.

```
**What didn't:**

**For next time:**
- [one concrete change]
```
