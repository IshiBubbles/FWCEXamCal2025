**Status:** Scaffold — populate as tests are encountered (Phase 2/3)

**Purpose:** Most engineering apprenticeship processes gate on an online test before a human ever reads the application. Knowing which test provider an employer uses is worth more than general preparation, because the formats are consistent and practisable.

## General preparation

- **Free practice:** most providers offer official practice tests — do those, not third-party imitations.
- **Numerical reasoning** is the most common format and the most practisable. Speed matters more than difficulty; the maths is GCSE-level but the clock is tight.
- **Situational judgement** tests are employer-values tests in disguise. Read the employer's stated values before sitting one.
- **Sit tests on a laptop, wired connection, no interruptions.** A timed-out test usually counts as a fail.

---

## Deadline interaction

Several tracked employers close when full rather than on a date (Airbus explicitly, BAE in practice). If an application requires a test, the test is part of the deadline — a form submitted in week one with the test outstanding may not count as complete. Check per employer.
