## 8. Aptitude test preparation
Most engineering apprenticeships screen with an online test before interview — typically numerical, mechanical and spatial reasoning. Fraser's A-level Physics is directly relevant to the mechanical reasoning sections. The common test publishers are **SHL** (Verify Mechanical Comprehension) and **Saville** (Swift Apprentice Aptitude).

|---|---|---|

| Practice Aptitude Tests — apprenticeships | https://www.practiceaptitudetests.com/industry/apprenticeship/ | Free tier |
| WikiJob — mechanical comprehension | https://www.wikijob.co.uk/aptitude-tests/test-types/mechanical-comprehension-test | Free |
| iPrep — mechanical aptitude | https://www.iprep.online/courses/mechanical-aptitude-test/ | Free tier |

## 9. Professional bodies
Neither lists vacancies — the value is content, credibility and (later) bursaries. Checked honestly:

| Body | URL | Reality check |
|---|---|---|
| **IET** | https://www.theiet.org/career/routes-to-engineering/apprenticeships | **Better of the two for Fraser right now.** Their "meet the companies" pages name IET-accredited apprenticeship employers (National Grid among them) — genuinely useful for judging scheme quality. Free membership generally starts **once he's an apprentice**. IET bursary is **£1,000/yr for up to 4 years** plus free membership — but only for those already on an IET-accredited scheme. |
| IMechE | https://www.imeche.org/membership-registration/become-a-member/affiliate-member/student-affiliate | Affiliate membership is **free for apprentices and undergrads**, but a 16+ A-level student **pays an annual fee**. Worth it mainly for Formula Student / Design Challenge / Railway Challenge access. |
| RAeS (Royal Aeronautical Society) | https://www.aerosociety.com/membership/ | Relevant to the aerospace and pilot tracks |
| Nuclear Institute | https://www.nuclearinst.com/Membership | Relevant if the nuclear route firms up |
