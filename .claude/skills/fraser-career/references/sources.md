# The four primary sources

## 1. Higherin — higherin.com
**The biggest gap in the setup, and still not signed up for.** Two things it does that
nothing else does: it aggregates the live "Register Your Interest — 2027" listings, and it
holds 48,000+ apprentice-written reviews, which is the only real signal on what a scheme is
like from the inside.

Absorbed **RateMyApprenticeship** (and RateMyPlacement) in August 2025 — same company now,
old links redirect. Do not maintain RateMyApprenticeship as a separate source.

Jobs: `higherin.com/search-jobs` · Reviews: `higherin.com/search-reviews`

Four of Fraser's five shortlisted roles came from here.

## 2. Gradcracker — gradcracker.com
Best single source for STEM and engineering. Fraser follows **93 employers** with alerts
active — the one registration that is genuinely done.

**Should be followed and is not: Babcock, GKN Aerospace, WSP, L3Harris.** All four are
either confirmed openers or strong local options.

Gradcracker follow is the *only* alert mechanism for BAE Systems, which offers no notify
option of its own.

## 3. GOV.UK Find an Apprenticeship — findapprenticeship.service.gov.uk
The official vacancy database. **England only**, which is fine for Southampton. Saved
searches by postcode and radius with email alerts.

Search Southampton, **25 miles**, all sectors, all levels. The old 15-mile radius missed
genuinely commutable roles.

This is where both WSP Southampton vacancies surfaced (`VAC1000346490` Planning Development,
`VAC1000300404` Building Structures) and Thames Water's L6 civil route
(`VAC2000020359`) — the most checkable identifiers in the whole dataset, because a VAC
reference either resolves or it does not.

## 4. Amazing Apprenticeships — amapps.uk
Publishes the **Higher & Degree Vacancy Listing three times per academic year: October,
January, April.** October is the bumper edition — October 2025 carried 1,600+ vacancies
across 50+ employers including EDF and MBDA.

The **Vacancy Snapshot** subscription at `amapps.uk/vacancies` is how that arrives
automatically. It is not subscribed. Until it is, the densest listing of the entire cycle
has to be remembered manually, in the month Fraser will be busiest.

Also useful: their step-by-step application guide, "get ready to apply" checklist, and
employer-led webinars.

## Secondary

- **UCAS Apprenticeships** — best filtering of any board (level, industry, distance,
  closing date). Smart Alerts is the single highest-value signup after Higherin.
- **Destination Nuclear Careers Portal** — nuclear roles often do not surface on the
  general GOV.UK sweep. Covers AWE, Sellafield, UKAEA, NDA, Sizewell C.
- **Springpod** — free virtual work experience, 6–10 hours, completion certificate that
  counts on applications. Do 2–3 before October.
- **Not Going To Uni**, **GetMyFirstJob** (employers can match *you*), **Indeed** (two
  saved searches at 25 miles), **ADS Group** apprenticeship directory (aerospace/defence,
  republished each October), **APTUS** (energy and power networks).

## Verification caveat

The network proxy blocks direct page loads for essentially every careers domain. Dates come
from search-result snippets *quoting* those pages, not from reading them, and snippets can
be cached. Label anything unverified, and say when a date needs confirming in a normal
browser.
